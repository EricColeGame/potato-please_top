## Reading Community Reports and Player Experience

Community chatter is useful, but it needs filtering. Player experience threads are great for finding workarounds and terrible for judging a game's overall quality, because unhappy players post more often than satisfied ones.

| Common Assumption | What Usually Happens |
|---|---|
| "The trailer shows everything" | Trailers highlight polished moments; reviews show the loop |
| "Early reviews are final" | Bugs get patched; the first-week score is often the lowest |
| "Negative review means bad game" | Many negatives are hardware-specific or about price |
| "Wishlisting does nothing" | It drives launch visibility and notifies you directly |
| "Sale prices arrive immediately" | Discounts typically follow once the release window settles |

A balanced reading strategy: skim the top three positive reviews, the top three negative reviews, and any pinned developer responses. If the negatives are about performance and the developer is replying with fixes, that's a good sign.
